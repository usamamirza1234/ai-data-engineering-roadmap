## 💡 Pro Tips

### Learning Strategy
1. **80/20 Rule:** Spend 80% time building projects, 20% watching tutorials
2. **Learn in Public:** Share your progress on LinkedIn/Twitter
3. **Join Communities:** Discord servers, Slack groups, Reddit
4. **Contribute to Open Source:** Great for portfolio and learning
5. **Build a Portfolio:** GitHub with 3-4 solid projects beats certificates

### Time Management
- **Pomodoro Technique:** 25 min focus, 5 min break
- **Morning Study:** Best retention before work
- **Weekend Projects:** Dedicate 6-8 hours on weekends
- **Consistency > Intensity:** 2 hours daily beats 14 hours on Sunday

### Career Tips
- **Network Early:** Connect with people before you need them
- **Document Everything:** Write READMEs, blog posts, LinkedIn updates
- **Practice Interviews:** Mock interviews on Pramp or Interviewing.io
- **Negotiate Offers:** Always negotiate, especially in Germany
- **Learn German:** Even B1 level helps significantly in Germany
